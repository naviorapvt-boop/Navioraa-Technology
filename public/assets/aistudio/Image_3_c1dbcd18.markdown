## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an approachable, dependable, and career-accelerating educational environment tailored for vocational computer training, diploma programs, and practical tech upskilling. Designed for college students, ambitious job seekers, and working professionals transitioning into software, design, and IT infrastructure, the interface balances institutional authority with welcoming accessibility.

The aesthetic follows **Corporate / Modern with High-Clarity Warmth**, steering entirely clear of dark futuristic SaaS tropes, heavy glassmorphism, or aggressive cyberpunk neons. Instead, the design leverages:
- Crisp, luminous white and off-white surfaces that promote readability, academic focus, and cognitive comfort.
- Deep, structured royal navy anchoring page hierarchy and navigation, establishing enduring reliability.
- Energetic vermilion-red brand accents and bright azure blue highlights for calls-to-action, status signals, and student achievement badges.
- Friendly, tactile affordances like pill-shaped categorical filters, rounded container cards, and subtle, airy box shadows that invite interaction without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for crystal-clear readability, trust, and enthusiastic educational momentum.

- **Primary (`#0B3B82`)**: Deep Royal Blue. Anchors primary branding, header titles, institutional highlights, and primary state markers. Conveys security, academic permanence, and professional pedigree.
- **Secondary (`#DA271F`)**: Signal Red / Vermilion. Drawn from the foundational institute identity, utilized strategically for high-priority CTA buttons (e.g., "Contact With Us", "Apply Now"), highlighted headline words, and active category filter states.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7`)**: Azure Blue. Drives digital course tags, tech-stack highlights, secondary CTAs ("Learn More"), and interactive link states.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B`)**: Slate Slate 800. Serves as the primary typographic color, delivering high-contrast readability that is softer and more organic on the eyes than absolute `#000000`.

### Backgrounds & Borders
- Canvas base relies on pure `#FFFFFF` alongside `#F8FAFC` for alternating content stripes and feature card foundations.
- Dividers and card frames use `#E2E8F0`, creating tactile container distinction without rigid harshness.
- Pure `#FFFFFF` cards resting on `#F8FAFC` backgrounds ensure visual separation through natural elevation and clean borders.

## Typography

The design system standardizes entirely on **Inter**, taking advantage of its geometric clarity, tall x-height, and neutral industrial efficiency across both high-impact promotional banners and dense syllabus course descriptions.

- **Section Eyebrows / Overlines**: Rendered in `badge-eyebrow` with uppercase transformation and spaced tracking (`0.08em`), housed inside soft-tinted pill badges or displayed above hero headers to structure topical hierarchy.
- **Compound Headlines**: Display titles frequently apply a dual-color emphasis pattern: base words in `#0B3B82` or `#1E293B`, paired with critical career keywords (e.g., "Choose Us?", "Skills Matter", "Courses") in `#DA271F`.
- **Course & Benefit Titles**: Always set in weights 600 or 700 to provide instant scanning for students navigating multiple career tracks.
- **Body & Editorial Copy**: Kept at balanced line heights (`1.5` to `1.65`) using muted slate tones (`#475569` or `#64748B`) to avoid visual fatigue across long curriculum outlines.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a responsive, 12-column fluid grid that locks to a maximum container width of `1280px` on desktop, ensuring focused sightlines for course directories and multi-column comparison tables.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapters
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` outer page margins. Course directories display in 3-column matrices (`span 4`), while why-choose-us trust points align in 2 or 4 columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters. Course grids reflow to 2 columns (`span 4`), sidebars collapse to inline drawers or top tab groups.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` safe-area canvas margins. Course cards and testimonial blocks reflow into full-width carousel modules or stacked 1-column layouts with finger-friendly horizontal snap-scrolling.

### Spacing Philosophy
Content blocks breathe with deliberate section-to-section padding (`space-2xl` on desktop, `space-xl` on mobile), giving academic offerings a clean, structured, and modern look that rejects cluttered legacy portal aesthetics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Layering combined with Low-Opacity Ambient Shadows**, creating soft physical separation that feels tactile yet light:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Default page backdrop (`#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`). No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` bordered by a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` stroke with an ambient drop shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(11, 59, 130, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`. The subtle royal blue undertone prevents the shadow from looking dirty or muddy.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Modules)**: Elevated on hover via a smooth transform `translateY(-4px)` with shadow bloom: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(11, 59, 130, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Navbars, Modals, Overlays)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` at 100% opacity, framed with `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`. No frosted glass or background blur is permitted; headers remain crisp, solid white to maximize contrast and text legibility.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced, approachable geometry defined by **Level 2 (Rounded)** for containers and intentional pill shapes for action components:

- **Cards & Enclosures**: Base radius is `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px), giving course previews, stat blocks, and feature tiles friendly, non-threatening silhouettes.
- **Action Buttons & Category Chips**: Formed as perfect circular pills (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to invite touch and denote immediate interactivity.
- **Input Fields & Text Areas**: Styled with `0.5rem` (8px) corners to preserve a crisp, reliable form layout while avoiding rigid, razor-sharp edges.
- **Icon Containers**: Key benefit icons reside within rounded rectangles (`0.75rem` radius) or soft circles tinted with ultra-light brand hues (`#FEE2E2` or `#E0F2FE`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), vermilion red background (`#DA271F`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.75rem 1.75rem` padding, font weight 600. On hover, shifts to `#B91C1C` with a slight elevation rise.
- **Secondary CTA**: Pill-shaped, deep royal blue (`#0B3B82`) or azure blue (`#0284C7`), text white. Ideal for secondary actions like "View Syllabus" or "Download Brochure".
- **Ghost / Outline Button**: Pill-shaped, border 1.5px solid `#0B3B82`, text `#0B3B82`, background transparent. Hover transforms background to `#0B3B82` with white text.

### Category Filter Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0.5rem 1.25rem`, typography `label-md`.
- **Default State**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, text `#475569`.
- **Active State**: Solid vermilion red (`#DA271F`) or royal blue (`#0B3B82`), text `#FFFFFF`, zero border, accompanied by a subtle tinted shadow.

### Course Cards
- Enclosed in `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border and Level 1 elevation.
- **Media Header**: 16:9 ratio course illustration or technology graphic featuring rounded top corners matching the card frame.
- **Badge Accents**: Floating or nested pill badges (e.g., "Most Popular", "100% Placement", "20+ Years Experience") tinted in `#FEE2E2` (text `#DA271F`) or `#FEF3C7` (text `#D97706`).
- **Card Footer**: Clear meta information including duration (e.g., "6 Months", "12 Months"), curriculum link (`Learn More →`), and gold star student ratings.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#FFFFFF` with `0.5rem` radius, framed with 1.5px solid `#CBD5E1`.
- Focused state highlights border with `#0B3B82` along with a soft `0 0 0 3px rgba(11, 59, 130, 0.15)` focus ring for accessible keyboard navigation.
- Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`, input text in high-contrast `#1E293B`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Precision 18px controls styled with `#0B3B82` when selected.
- Radio buttons use a clean concentric white/blue ring; checkboxes feature a crisp white check mark against a solid blue or red fill.

### Metric & Trust Highlights (Why Choose Us)
- Clean vertical card with centered or left-aligned tinted icon containers (36px–48px), bold title (`headline-sm`), and concise two-line value proposition in `body-sm`.
- Numbered milestone cards featuring bold red numeric markers (`#DA271F` background, white label) to emphasize hiring stats, salary increases, and industry certifications.
